About

Hey! I am a college student with a home gym dream and a financing problem: hold prices skyrocket past hundreds just for basic sets. So, I put my years of woodworking classes to test, dug into my favorite piles of scrap wood in a friend's garage, and emerged with ZZ Collective. My hope is to provide my communities with low-cost holds — $5–$7 a piece — to support visions bigger than my own. We are currently in the process of fabricating our first inventory stock. Until then, hold tight until drop-day! Sincerely,
Lila Gizzie, Founder
